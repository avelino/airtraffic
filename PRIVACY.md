# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-22_

Air Traffic is a browser extension for Chrome and Firefox. It routes tabs into tab groups (Chrome) or containers (Firefox) based on URL rules you write.

## What the extension reads

To decide where a tab belongs, Air Traffic reads the URL of a tab when it navigates and compares it against your rules. This happens entirely inside your browser.

## What the extension stores

- Your rules, destinations and settings, in the browser's extension storage (`storage.local`).
- If you turn on "Sync rules across devices", your rules and destinations are stored in `storage.sync`, which your browser syncs through your own browser account (Google account on Chrome, Firefox account on Firefox). Air Traffic does not operate that service.

URLs you visit are never stored, logged or kept after the rule check.

## What the extension sends

Nothing. Air Traffic makes no network requests, has no analytics or telemetry, and does not share, sell or transfer any data to anyone.

## Export and import

The export feature writes your rules and settings to a JSON file that you save yourself. Import reads a file you choose. Neither leaves your machine unless you move the file.
